Everything you need to talk to the people you love

A messenger designed for Africa and its diaspora: your languages, your loved ones, your privacy.

Awa writes in French James reads in English
1 · Translation

Everyone writes in their own language

Tap the translation icon, choose the language you receive your messages in, then the one your friend will read them in. That's it.

  • English, French, Wolof, Fula, Lingala, Swahili, Bambara, Hausa, Yoruba, Arabic, Portuguese…
  • Each chat keeps its own languages
  • You can correct a translation
🔒 Only the two of you can read it
2 · Privacy

Your chats stay between you

Messages are end-to-end encrypted: nobody else can read them, not even Tam-Tam. And for your statuses, you decide who sees them.

  • My friends: all your friends see the status for 24 hours
  • Private: only the people you tick can see it
  • View-once messages, locked chats
📅 One year ago today…
3 · Together

Your memories, your loved ones

Keep your photos and videos in your Memories, sorted by year with their real date: Tam-Tam reminds you of them every year. Find your loved ones by their username, without ever giving out your phone number.

  • Public, private (with a code) or shared memories
  • Search by @username
  • Your number stays private
⚽ I'm going to the match!
4 · Location

Always reassured

Your child is off to a football match with friends? They share their location with you, continuously and encrypted. You know they got there safely.

  • You choose who to share your location with
  • Stop sharing at any time
  • Even the server cannot read the location
