🌍 Tam-Tam Language Ambassadors

Tam-Tam is a free, end-to-end encrypted messenger that translates messages and keeps African mother tongues alive. Write in English, and your friend reads you in Wolof, Lingala or Swahili, and the other way round.

Tam-Tam follows the spirit of the International Decade of Indigenous Languages (2022-2032), proclaimed by the United Nations and coordinated by UNESCO.

What an ambassador does

  • corrects the translations Tam-Tam suggests in your language; a correction confirmed by several members becomes the shared translation for everyone;
  • spreads the word about Tam-Tam around you and in your community;
  • tells us about the words, expressions and turns of phrase that belong to your language.

It is voluntary, with no time commitment, and you do it from your phone.

What Tam-Tam offers you

  • the “🌍 Ambassador” badge and your language, shown on your profile;
  • your name on the app’s acknowledgements page;
  • early access to new features;
  • a certificate of contribution, useful for students, teachers and translators.

Become an ambassador

Write to ngossodesire@gmail.com with your name, your mother tongue, your country and the Gmail address you will use Tam-Tam with. We will send you an invitation.
